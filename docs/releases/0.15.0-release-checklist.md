# 0.15.0: manual check before release

The 15-minute run of both journeys in the installed desktop app (workflow plan §9, "Real app").


Against the real Task Mentor (staging account), on macOS and Windows:
1. Launch signed in as a student: Home is in front, Next up is right, Start/Continue opens the work and the brief.
2. Submit; close the toast only after 10 s. Have a teacher return it: the notice arrives, the brief shows Returned with Submit Again, the side bar and status bar agree.
3. Have it graded: the score is at the top of the brief.
4. Launch signed in as a teacher: Grading is in the activity bar at once; Home's Start Grading works.
5. Turn on Do Not Disturb and start a practice exam: the time warnings still show.
6. Settings › Startup Editor: none, relaunch: no Home.
7. Student: the side bar shows one line; the brief has the only Save and Submit; Submit, then Withdraw to Edit.
8. Student: open a quiz practical from Assignments; with the attempt closed, Open Quiz comes first; start, submit.
9. Signed out, choose New Project: sign in in the browser; TMCode carries on.
10. Teacher: Home › Start Grading opens the oldest submission with the side bar folded; Feedback is visible without scrolling; ⌘Enter saves and moves on; the split button remembers Save Draft.
11. Turn Wi-Fi off and save: the message is in plain words, with Details.
12. Resize the window to about 1000 px wide: "…" appears in the title bar and opens Help.
13. Student: the coach mark shows once; Assignments is the first icon; no Source Control in an assignment workspace.
14. Teacher: Assignments shows Teaching first with Grade (n); Close Review is at the top of the grading tab.
15. Get graded, close the message, restart TMCode: the centre still lists it, with View Feedback.
